# Justice: dev.66 implementation and module contract

Authorized 2026-10-31. This is a new `mam_justice.*` / `mam_jus_*` module. The removed `mam_cj_*` investigation/trial/register is not restored. Source implementation and engine acceptance are separate: see [JUSTICE-TESTING.md](JUSTICE-TESTING.md) and the generated [JUSTICE-VERIFICATION.json](JUSTICE-VERIFICATION.json).

Final source verification, 2026-10-31: the full isolated builder passed all ten suites and published only final changed files. Justice has 21 runtime text/localization files, 12 events (including the hidden native worker), 165 parsed definitions, 166 English keys and 14 audited native definitions. All 23 Justice contracts and 13 Censor tests passed; exact native acceptance/cost parity, all 159 protected files and fiscal/project/scheduler/jurisdiction checks passed. No installed vanilla file was edited. Engine execution, actual native callback behavior, UI rendering, migration and long-run AI balance remain untested. No crash fix is claimed.

## What each role does

| Role | Entry and discretion |
|---|---|
| Justice | Receives senior judicial business automatically; hears the accused, considers ministerial advocacy, requests a Censor inspection, proposes custody, mercy or a lawful punishment; can refuse unsupported prosecution |
| Censor | Refers an inspection without deciding guilt; independently investigates actual judicial solicitation or recorded unauthorized conduct; retains administrative censure/remonstrance |
| Accused | Answers, challenges the grounds, asks for mercy, offers genuine surrender, refuses/reports a private demand, and may petition once |
| Connected minister | May defend an official, support scrutiny, or seek imperial procedural review |
| Chancellor | Coordinates delayed business and petitions; cannot invent grounds, give the imperial seal, erase evidence or reverse a completed native action |
| Emperor | Controls Judicial Authority law; reviews reserved measures, issues/withdraws identifiable warrants, may supervise or refuse a proposal, or rebuke its actual abusive author |
| Actual custodian/right-holder | Consents when a proposed native measure requires that person's own participation; the court cannot silently borrow another character's prison or pardon authority |

New intake concerns native criminal grounds or actual detention of kingdom/territorial-empire governors and central ministers. No county/duchy routine case spam; no new random accusations or automatic crimes. Co-rulers and the sovereign are outside this module's subject filter. A personally identified proceeding survives office loss while its subject remains an eligible person in the Chinese realm. Recently retired subjects stay protected from bypassing senior authority through a native Justice interaction; explicit Justice/imperial follow-up is possible, but never replays an old disposition.

Ministry interactions are **Justice: Refer a Judicial Matter** and **Justice: Petition for Review**. **Justice: Resume My Pending Business** belongs only to the current addressed reviewer. An accused player also has **Justice: Petition for Review of My Treatment** when eligible, including in custody, so self-petition does not depend on the character menu supporting self-interactions. Both petition routes use the same once-only helper. Native Justice council tasks, Find Criminal scheme and native costs/rewards remain the foundation; there is no additional reward for clicking an approval.

## Proceedings and meaningful discretion

One sovereign weekly service admits at most one fresh native-ground/detention episode per poll. Up to eight institutional proceedings wait; one actionable modal desk lease is shared across them. Most handoffs take 7 days. A subject is not repeatedly re-filed merely because an unchanged native reason persists. A separate Censor referral can introduce actual new material. A retained prisoner receives at most one automatic follow-up after 180 days. A case has a three-year outer limit, not a conviction-by-timeout.

Stages are 1 initial Justice decision, 2 accused's answer, 3 ministerial intervention, 4 disposition, 5 imperial review, 6 actual performer's consent, 7 private proposal, 8 coordination/appeal, 9 explicit unauthorized noncapital choices, 10 prisoner banishment terms, 11 quiet native commitment, 12 bounded Censor inquiry wait, 13 native player execution-notice wait, and 90 result correspondence. Stages 11–13 have no modal Justice event. Censor inquiry waits at most 60 days. Defer is a single 30-day choice; a delayed matter can reach coordination once. A player who leaves a modal unanswered can recall it; its lease can be renewed after 90 days. Successor/stage tokens invalidate stale choices.

Traits influence real choices: compassionate/forgiving officials favor mercy, diligent/just officials favor scrutiny, greedy/deceitful/ambitious officials may solicit private benefit, and vengeful/sadistic officials favor harsh handling. Friends, family and rivalries affect discretion. Honest/Just AI cannot select private solicitation or the explicit bypass. Player choices remain available with relevant native stress consequences. Unauthorized capital punishment and banishment have no bypass. Restraint blocks private demands/bypass and requires imperial review for ordinary senior measures.

An accused's Diplomacy and Learning influence persuasive mitigation. Deceitful presentation additionally pits Intrigue against the judge's Intrigue; the judge's Learning reduces uncritical acceptance. The chance is bounded 10–80%, rolled once per case, and affects AI mercy weights rather than legal truth. A high-Diplomacy advocate gives additional weight to an actual mercy intervention. Justice's Learning influences scrutiny and requesting surrender. Neither a good speech nor a successful skill roll creates/removes a criminal reason. Player judges still choose.

## Actual powers and technical limits

Actions are 1 arrest, 2 native pardon, 3 release, 4 execution, 5 native banishment, 6 withdraw this module's own warrant, 7 issue a documented imperial warrant. The current Judicial Authority law delegates ordinary senior custody/pardon/release or reserves all senior measures. Grave measures, central ministers, protected imperial family, supervised cases and severe pardons are always imperial matters. The direct native Justice UI and native Find Criminal event have matching availability and commitment gates. Other actors' native powers and lower-level native business remain native.

CK3 exposes no verified script command to send/accept an arbitrary interaction. The bridge therefore compiles **pinned, exact original native acceptance bodies and costs**, binds the actual performer and native option defaults, and validates eligibility before charging. A hidden character worker on the actual performer supplies the correct ROOT to indirect native helpers; an institutional story cannot stand in for that character. Temporary native scopes are cleared before binding a new action, preventing old strife/faction flags from leaking between proceedings. Native automatic acceptance or genuine surrender is required for arrest. Native banishment requires the actual prisoner's consent; ordinary release has no special coerced terms selected. Native execution/release effects retain all original personal consequences and succession. This is not an invented successful arrest, fake death, or hand-written title transfer.

An AI executioner gives a player the original native execution notice (`char_interaction.0170`) before its final option causes death. Justice waits quietly for that actual execution, verifies the execution death reason and actual killer, and never replays the notice, fee or punishment. A different death or 90-day timeout ends the custom wait without claiming success. The native notice itself remains native and is not silently replaced by an immediate kill.

**Pardon is broad, but not universal.** The native Justice pardon consumes the immediate liege's imprisonment, execution, banishment and revocation rights together. A non-Justice pardon consumes that actor's own bundle. The module selects an actual affected authority, names it, and uses the emperor personally if only imperial rights are relevant; that requires the imperial seal. Another right-holder's own participation requires consent. A reserved approval records the authority and current reason-category mask; changed authority/categories require renewed review. CK3 does not expose individual offense identities within that native bundle: an added offense in the same category cannot be distinguished. Tooltips explicitly authorize the full current bundle, never one selected charge. Another authority may retain independent rights; pardon does not itself release a prisoner.

**Custom findings need a separate explicit warrant.** A dated, corroborated paid appointment, detected knowingly false official certification, actual recorded judicial solicitation, or actual unauthorized judicial coercion can support the emperor's decision. Accusation, detention, friendship, a legitimate expense, a normal native pardon or a native Loyalty Hook is not that evidence. Custom offenses are prospective from `h_china.mam_jus_statute_started`; older financial/certification records remain administrative information. The warrant is an identifiable native opinion modifier with `imprisonment_reason = yes`, lasting three years. It grants no execution, banishment or revocation right. The actual source identity is consumed once in `mam_jus_warrant_sources`; opening another inspection cannot recycle it. Custody, clemency, expiration or a new reign cannot regenerate that source's spent right.

Selective clemency removes only `mam_jus_imperial_warrant` from its actual issuer. It leaves unrelated native rights and existing custody intact. This solves the single-custom-warrant case without claiming the engine can selectively pardon an arbitrary native offense. Issuer death/sovereign change retires the old warrant; no sovereign inherits a fabricated renewed right.

The selected warrant basis is canonical, not just whichever source pointer happens to exist: an eligible paid-appointment record, then an eligible detected certification, then an eligible judicial record. Display, proposal identity, approval and source-consumption use those same actual eligibility predicates. An unproved secondary record cannot consume the warrant identity. The proposal and imperial seal retain that exact source identity; changed grounds require a new proposal/review rather than silently authorizing another offense. The issued warrant retains its actual source snapshot separately from later display bindings.

## Money, consequences and responsibility

Every native resource cost is read from the pinned game source, then checked on the actual performer. Public Treasury is debited and verified before other costs; native acceptance is executed once. Affordability is not rechecked after the debit, which would incorrectly strand a paid fee. A failed Treasury debit creates no matching income, no Gold substitute and no success. A Treasury-short Justice minister can request the existing Revenue kind-12 operating advance, quietly; Revenue's actual reserve/approval rules still apply. Retries are bounded, and 90 days without affordability returns the matter for another decision rather than hanging indefinitely.

Native UI/scheme actions retain their own engine-handled costs/rewards; observers only settle obsolete custom proposals and identifiable warrant state. They never charge again. Actual outcomes are checked before a success receipt. A realm transaction lease pauses reentrant Justice workers during native death/title/custody callbacks. An interrupted lease is quarantined after two days; no action, fee or reward is replayed.

A corrupt minister can propose 100 **personal Gold** or an explicit personal favor for an actually completed pardon/release. It is never Treasury. The recipient can refuse or report it. No benefit moves on proposal/approval alone; the actual proposer must still hold Justice at commitment. A native Loyalty Hook created by the actual pardon can fulfill the agreed obligation without adding a second hook. The original native hook alone is never labeled corrupt. Reports/actual breaches can request Censor scrutiny; an unreported real demand can be found using the Censor/Justice detection skills already implemented. Neither the report nor a detected receipt automatically convicts the author.

An unreported private demand is shown only to its actual participants. It does not grant the emperor an omniscient rebuke option. Imperial handling requires an actual report, public recorded unauthorized conduct, or a corroborated Censor memorial that has actually reached the throne. Source receipts are read without rewriting their historical facts. Event evidence paragraphs distinguish the actual supported appointment, certification or judicial record rather than describing every hypothetical offense as present.

Imperial rebuke costs the actual living recorded author 75 Influence and a two-year nonstacking restraint (-10% monthly Influence, -5 General Opinion). It does not punish a successor, undo completed clemency, replay an execution, or restore consumed rights. A one-time petition concerning a completed action on a living subject is **receipt/handling review only**; it cannot return to disposition or replay that action. A pending accepted native execution notice cannot be interrupted/replayed through the petition helper. Scrutiny of the judicial author's actual conduct remains available to the Censor after a subject's death.

Private settlement records the actual Gold paid or hook fulfilled. An agreed Gold payment becoming unaffordable after the native act is marked unpaid; it is never silently converted into a hook. Viewing the bypass menu itself does not charge trait stress; choosing the unauthorized proposal does, in addition to native consequences of an actually executed act.

## Ownership, generation and compatibility

Sources: `tools/build_justice.py`, `tools/build_justice_events.py`, `tools/templates/justice/`. Full staged `tools/build_common_fund.py` generates Justice after Censor and runs `tools/verify_justice.py`. Never generate/edit runtime alone. `docs/JUSTICE-NATIVE-BOUNDARIES.json` pins 14 deliberately audited native definitions, including the deferred execution notice/helper; do not refresh it to excuse source drift. `JUSTICE-NATIVE-COMPILER.json` reports acceptance hashes/resources. Four native interactions and Find Criminal event 8071 are narrow overrides; compatibility with other mods changing these same definitions requires comparison/load-order testing.

Realm title owns queue, desk, service, statute date and source-consumption lists. Stories own cases and immutable actual actor/cost receipts. The current sovereign owns story lifetimes and transfers ownership on death; the reviewer is temporary. Subject identity and actual personal authors never transfer with a title. No Personnel appointment/custody/score/mark/patronage or Revenue authority definitions are rewritten. Native consequences use their original callbacks.

Existing saves adopt the law/service through startup, quarterly recovery or sovereign/Justice title gain. They do not receive invented historical crimes, catch-up costs or retroactive convictions. Newly declared story/law/interaction classes require a restarted game session for engine acceptance; preserving developer hot reload does not prove every category updates safely. Source verification is not crash, visual, AI or long-run gameplay acceptance.
